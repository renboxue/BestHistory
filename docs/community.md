# Zhiban Community

**BestHistory is a Best Series product by Zhiban.**

Zhiban Community is the shared community for people who use BestHistory and future products from Zhiban.

Use it to:

- talk about BestHistory and other Best Series products;
- report bugs and suggest features;
- join beta tests and early-access programs;
- follow important product releases and announcements;
- help shape what Zhiban builds next.
