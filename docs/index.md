<section class="bh-section bh-problem">
  <div class="bh-section-heading">
    <span class="bh-kicker">THE REAL PROBLEM</span>
    <h2>You remember what the website did. You just don't remember what it was called.</h2>
    <p>Most history tools assume you know the date, the page title, or the exact keyword. Real memory is messier.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bh-grid bh-grid-3">
    <div class="bh-card"><strong>“That PDF tool…”</strong><p>You used it two months ago. The tab is gone. The name is gone. You only remember what you used it for.</p></div>
    <div class="bh-card"><strong>“That AI site…”</strong><p>You tried several similar tools. One was actually useful, but now they all blur together.</p></div>
    <div class="bh-card"><strong>“That page I meant to keep…”</strong><p>You keep dozens of tabs open because closing them feels like losing them forever.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="bh-section">
  <div class="bh-section-heading">
    <span class="bh-kicker">THE DIFFERENCE</span>
    <h2>History organized around how people remember.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="bh-grid bh-grid-3">
    <div class="bh-card bh-feature"><span class="bh-num">01</span><h3>Website-first history</h3><p>Instead of opening a wall of thousands of page visits, start with the websites you actually used. Then drill down into pages only when you need them.</p></div>
    <div class="bh-card bh-feature"><span class="bh-num">02</span><h3>AI Recall for fuzzy memory</h3><p>Describe what you vaguely remember. AI Recall helps turn that memory into better search clues, then verifies them against your local history.</p></div>
    <div class="bh-card bh-feature"><span class="bh-num">03</span><h3>Private when it should be private</h3><p>Private Mode keeps selected history encrypted locally and can optionally remember incognito-window visits without mixing them into normal history.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="bh-section bh-split">
  <div><span class="bh-kicker">YOUR OWN MEMORY LAYER</span><h2>Tag it. Describe it. Find it the way you remember it.</h2><p>A website's official title is often not the thing you remember. BestHistory lets you add your own tags and notes — like “the site I used to turn PDFs into images” — and search those later.</p><p>You can also sort by recent use, frequent use, name, pinned sites, unorganized sites, trash, and private sites.</p></div>
  <div class="bh-shot"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/renboxue/BestHistory/main/assets/screenshots/site-detail.webp" alt="BestHistory website details, notes and tags"></div>

Expand them when you need detail; keep them folded when you just want to remember the flow.</p></div>
</section>

AI features only send the limited information needed for the request when you deliberately use them.</p></div>
